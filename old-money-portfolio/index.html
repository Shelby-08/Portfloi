About

From the hills of Sirmaur to a first year at Newton School of Technology.

Nahan, a small town in the Sirmaur district of Himachal Pradesh, is where I come from. I finished Class 10 at Carmel Convent Senior Secondary School, then moved to Career Academy for Class 12 in the Non-Medical stream.

My first real code was in Java, which I learned through Classes 9 and 10, working up to object-oriented programming and sorting. In Class 11 I built a small number guessing game, my first project of my own.

Today I am a first-year student at Newton School of Technology, where I am learning Python, and I have recently become a consultant at WorldQuant. This page is where I will keep a record of what I build next.
